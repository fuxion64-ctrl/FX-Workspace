# 1.4.6 - Settings inventory (stage 4, input for the redesign)

Every row in Settings today, read from index.html (3 Nov 2026): **130 rows on 18 pages**. Marked here is what reads
as clutter from a user's side. Nothing is removed yet - the keep / merge / remove decisions are the redesign (4.3,
at xhigh), and you approve the table before anything changes.

Marks: **dup** the same thing in two places · **move** in the wrong place · **default** a technical choice that
could be one good behaviour · **tool** a button or test, not a setting · **legacy** an old-or-new choice.

## Technical choices that could be one good default

- **Voice (23 rows, the biggest page):** Listener (English), Whisper model, Model for other languages, GPU listener,
  Compact voice model, Pause between sentences, Wait after you stop, Microphone sensitivity, Quick replies.
- **Agent (14):** Load tools as needed, Revisions after review, Raise effort for complex tasks, Show planning in the
  chat, Cross-check, Check answers, Compress a long chat at.
- **Images (7):** Step cache, Room kept for drawing, Keep the engine ready.
- **Web search (14):** Built-in browser, Use the browser when blocked, Reuse results for, Keep search history + Keep
  it for, SEC contact.
- **Appearance:** Write replies in (fade), Phosphor at night, Opacity / Click-through / Reset positions (Overlay).

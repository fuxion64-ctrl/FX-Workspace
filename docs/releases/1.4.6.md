# FX Workspace 1.4.6

A polish release before 1.5.0: things that were broken now work, tool replies are faster, the looks are redrawn, and the app now tells you when an update is out.

**Updating:** from 1.4.2 or later, Settings › About › **Check now** › **Update now**, or press **Update** on Home when it offers one. From older versions, run `FX-Workspace-Setup-1.4.6.exe` over them. Your chats, settings, keys and models stay as they are.

## Fixed

- **Renaming a chat works:** the title becomes a text box right where it is. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
- **The mouse's Back button** no longer leaves the app stuck on "Starting…". It closes whatever is on top instead.
- **The System panel shows real numbers:** CPU isn't stuck at 0%, and the GPU no longer reads "Unavailable" while it's busy.
- **Harmless chats aren't locked any more:** "make the return explicit" in a coding chat no longer closes it, and a locked chat has a visible Review and reopen button.
- **One moving signal:** only the newest mark moves during a reply. A Research turn used to move three at once.
- A local model is told which of its extra tool calls weren't run yet, so it no longer thinks they ran.
- Reopening the app right after quitting no longer says the server stopped while starting.

## Faster

- **"Write me a function / a poem / an email"** is answered in the chat. It no longer gets the run-code tool or asks to run what you only wanted to read (13 s → 7 s on Qwen 3.5 4B).
- **Quick questions about your PC** ("what's using my PC?", "list the files in …") are about 3× faster on Auto effort, because a one-step look doesn't need a written plan and a review (15 s → 5 s on Qwen 3.5 4B). Changes still get both.
- **Big model downloads** use several connections at once (a 644 MB file: about 10 minutes before, 12 s now), and resume where they stopped.
- **Tune speed** lets you keep chatting: it pauses for you and carries on by itself.

## Pictures

- Images lists only the picture models you have. The rest are under "Not on this PC".
- **Add your own picture model:** an SD 1.x, 2.x or SDXL checkpoint (.safetensors or .gguf).

## Settings, sorted

- **Nine pages by goal:** Appearance, Models, Assistant, Memory, Web and privacy, Connections, Data and files, Diagnostics, About. Search finds any setting.
- **Storage** (a new page): each drive the app uses, how full it is, and what's on it - chat models, model files nothing uses, picture, video and music models, the llama.cpp engine, what you made.
- **Hide my IP** is at the top of Search and network, and a small **Tor** tag in the top bar shows when traffic goes through Tor.
- Quieter and tidier everywhere: straight lines between groups, models whose file is gone fold away at the end, short paths, no red until you confirm a delete.

## The looks

- **Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini redrawn** from public design write-ups of their apps (inspired by them, not affiliated): Claude's cream and coral, ChatGPT's rounder white, Grok's dark with pill buttons, Gemini's pale blue-grey with pills.
- **Eclipse has its own night:** a light, wide greeting, outlined chrome, and the corona lit only while it works.
- **Each look has its own thinking mark** for each kind of work. The Claude look's is a small 3D scene (Regular) or soft liquid clay (Liquid), set in Settings › Appearance.
- **Want the old ones back?** Settings › Appearance › Extras: "Looks redrawn in 1.4.6 → Original" and "Thinking mark → Orbs or Legacy".
- The places (Images, Videos, Music, Projects, Docs…) are cleaner: one main action, sentence-case labels, the page name shown once.

## New

- **Updates on Home:** when a new version is out, Home offers it with Update, What's new and Not now. Turn it off in Settings › About.
- **The assistant can switch the look:** "switch to dark mode" or "use the Claude look".
- MossadGPT's glow around the incognito button is now in Aurora and Eclipse too.
